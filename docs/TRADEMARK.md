## What you may do without asking

- State truthfully that your software is built on, derived from, or compatible
  with Reelsi — for example: "a fork of Reelsi", "powered by Reelsi",
  "compatible with Reelsi project files".
- Use the name in articles, tutorials, reviews, talks, and comparisons.
- Keep the name in an unmodified copy that you redistribute as-is.

## What requires written permission

- Naming your fork "Reelsi" or anything confusingly similar.
- Using the logo as the icon or branding of your own product or service.
- Using the name or logo in a way that suggests the author endorses, sponsors,
  or maintains your product.
- Registering domains, social accounts, app-store listings, or package names
  built around "Reelsi".

## If you fork

Forks are welcome — that is the point of the license. Please give your fork its
own name and its own logo, and describe it as "based on Reelsi" rather than as
Reelsi itself. This keeps bug reports and support requests flowing to the right
place, which protects both of us.
